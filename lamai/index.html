What Nova can do

General knowledge

Ask questions like "Who is Albert Einstein?" or "Explain photosynthesis". Nova searches online (using your browser) and reads a concise answer aloud.

Open apps & sites

Say "Open YouTube", "Open Gmail", or "Open Facebook" and Nova will launch them in a new tab.

WhatsApp messages

Say something like "Send WhatsApp message to Alex saying I am on my way". Nova will prepare a WhatsApp link with the message filled in so you can confirm and send.

Voice + text output

Every response is spoken with text shown in the conversation panel, so you can read or listen.

Built in your browser using the Web Speech API and simple automations. Microphone access is required for voice input.
